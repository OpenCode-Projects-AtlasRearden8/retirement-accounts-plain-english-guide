Print this page, fill it in with a pen, and stick it somewhere you’ll see it. Every blank below points at a number that lives in your plan’s website or an HR email — no spreadsheets, no research.

7. Standing reminders (set these as calendar alerts)

  • Check my account: every ______ months
  • Raise my contribution 1% when: __________________ (next raise / every January)
  • Beneficiary named and current: ☐ Yes ☐ Needs updating (2 minutes on the plan site)

Answer key, in one line each

  • Can’t find the match formula? Email HR: “Could you tell me my plan’s employer match formula and my vesting schedule?”
  • Money sitting as cash, invested 0%? Log in → “Investment options” → pick the target-date fund for your retirement year → direct 100% of contributions there.
  • Old 401(k) doing nothing? Request a direct rollover to an IRA or your new plan — see rollovers. Never cash out.
