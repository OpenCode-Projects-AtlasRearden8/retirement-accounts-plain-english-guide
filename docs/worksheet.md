### Answer key, in one line each

- **Can't find the match formula?** Email HR: *"Could you tell me my plan's employer match formula and my vesting schedule?"*
- **Money sitting as cash, invested 0%?** Log in → "Investment options" → pick the target-date fund for your retirement year → direct 100% of contributions there.
- **Old 401(k) doing nothing?** Request a *direct rollover* to an IRA or your new plan — see [rollovers](rollovers.md). Never cash out.
